Market Trend Respect Engine

Scans all 28 majors on H1 + M15. Per-pair Trend Respect is a continuous weighted average over the last 10 candles: 35% Price Position (bell curve on EMA20 distance), 25% EMA Stack (ATR-normalised), 40% EMA20 Slope (regression). TPI = 1 − σ(Respect) / 100. MDE (Market Directional Efficiency) = mean per-pair |net move| / |total path| over the last 20 candles — the true trend-vs-chop discriminator. Market Health = 0.25 MTI + 0.20 Breadth + 0.15 Agreement + 0.10 TPI + 0.30 MDE.
