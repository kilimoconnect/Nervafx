Market Trend Respect Engine

Movement-based MTRE. EMA20 vs EMA50 sets direction. Over the last 20 candles, sum the price movement of structural breaks in the trend direction (higher-high for BUY, lower-low for SELL) and the counter direction. Pair Respect = trendMove / (trendMove + counterMove) × 100. Market MTRE splits BUY-side and SELL-side averages so the dominant regime is visible directly.
