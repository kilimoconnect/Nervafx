Three independently-tracked strategies share one configurable engine: period comparison → top-3 pairs → confirm direction → entry trigger → publish → track outcome → performance. Pair selection ranks all 28 pairs by the absolute difference of the two currencies' period log returns (from the zero-sum 1/8 index). The day, week and month baselines start at 00:00 EAT (UTC+3). Confirmation uses the latest completed H1/H4/D1 candle (return + 5-of-7 breadth). Entry is a 3-candle pattern on M15/H1/H4 with a Wilder-ATR(14) stop; target 2R; max hold 16 entry candles. Execution is estimated (mid-price only, no bid/ask): spread, slippage and financing are modelled costs; intrabar stops/targets are monitored on M5 where available (≈2026-04 onward) else M15, stop-first on an ambiguous bar. Account return = marked-to-market equity, risk 0.5%/trade. All figures are MODELLED and UNVALIDATED — not trade signals, probabilities, or real results.
