No stops, targets, sizing or win-rate claims; a trigger close is a reference price, not a fill.

Currency strength is a ±1 base/quote vote per pair; a currency needs all seven legs known to qualify (an unknown leg is not a neutral vote), and with complete data the eight scores sum to zero. A pair qualifies only when a strong currency is paired against a weak one; a signal is confirmed only when a fresh trigger-timeframe break agrees with that direction and the pair still qualifies at the trigger candle's close.
